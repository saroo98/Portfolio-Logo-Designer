About A Note

A quiet practice, patiently kept.

Maryam Ansari has been drawing logos since 2010 — based in the UK. Wordmarks, monograms, mascots, pictorial marks — whichever the brief honestly asks for, nothing more.

The work prizes proportion, restraint, and the willingness to redraw a single line until it sits right. Tools change; that part doesn't.
